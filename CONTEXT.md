# Prisma editorial domain

Prisma organises citizens' access to political programmes through reviewed summaries, with evidence and an explicit editorial review process.

## Language

**Electoral formation**:
A party, coalition or other electoral organisation whose programme is represented in the portal. A coalition is not interchangeable with each of its constituent parties.
_Avoid_: Treating one formation as one constituency candidature.

**Candidature**:
An electoral list or set of candidates presented for a particular election and constituency. Counting candidatures does not count distinct programmes.

**Programme**:
The official set of proposals a formation publishes for a particular election. It can span several documents or web pages.
_Avoid_: Substituting campaign announcements or programmes from another election.

**Source version**:
A preserved version of an official programme document consulted during review. The same URL may serve different versions at different times.

**Extracted segment**:
A localisable portion of source text, corresponding to a physical page or a document section. Its evidence remains distinct from the wording of a summary.

**Policy area**:
A shared thematic category used to organise proposals across formations, such as housing or education.
_Avoid_: Using policy to mean both a category and an individual proposal.

**Synthesis**:
A concise selection of a formation's principal proposals in a policy area. Completing its review does not mean cataloguing every proposal in the programme.

**Synthesis point**:
An individually identifiable statement in a synthesis, supported by source evidence. It must preserve the proposal's scope and conditions.

**Editorial review**:
The process of verifying provenance, fidelity and interpretation, debating evidence and agreeing on a publishable version. Agreement alone does not establish factual truth.

**Suggestion**:
A citizen's proposed correction or question about portal content. It may identify an error, express a preference, duplicate existing work or refer to an older version.

**Triage**:
The review of a suggestion to agree on a correction, follow-up or reasoned closure. A proposed outcome without consensus remains pending.

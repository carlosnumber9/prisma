# Portal-scoped content

Status: accepted on 22 November 2026.

Keep only reviewed programme summaries, displayed formation information, provincial Congress presence and the evidence needed to maintain those views. Politician profiles, individual affiliations and offices, candidate names/order, substitutes, Senate lists and historical research archives are outside Prisma's product scope. Unapproved work stays outside Git. This replaces the previous nationwide individual-candidature research remit and prevents future collection without a concrete portal consumer.

Territorial programme associations are declared in `content/editorial/programme-identities.json` under the [provincial identity rules](provincial-data.md#programme-identity-and-geography). They do not merge electoral legal identities or assign a coalition member's programme automatically.

## Programme coverage limits

EAJ-PNV's complete 43-page consulted programme is represented by 83 points covering all 225 numbered commitments and actionable introductory material. Partido Autónomos' complete 15-page programme is represented by 75 points covering all 100 numbered measures and their operative implementation clauses. Both official PDF hashes were reverified on 25 November 2026. Escaños en Blanco's whole live programme and linked [manifesto](https://escanos.org/conocenos/manifiesto/) are represented by six institutional points; its other seven areas were reviewed without applicable proposals. The retained 22 November HTML versions identify the consulted bytes; current bodies were compared semantically on 25 November without asserting byte identity for the dynamic pages.

Soria ¡YA!'s 24 points cover the full HTML programme section and its one-page companion; pensions were reviewed without an applicable proposal. Geroa Bai's 15 points cover both 14-page language versions; housing, healthcare and employment have no distinct actionable measures beyond diagnoses or general public-service commitments represented in social protection. Tercera Edad en Acción's 46 points cover all thirteen programme sections and implementation commitments, preserving the conditional pension, tax and evaluation provisions. Their original hashes and exact supporting quotations were independently checked.

Iustitia Europa's 42 points cover all fifteen sections of its 28-page contract, including operative introductory material, after comparison with all fifteen linked web chapters. Material differences in the PDF and web wording and the ambiguous land-law/expropriation fragment remain explicit in the affected points' maintenance notes; the synthesis does not infer an unstated legal instrument. All eight areas contain reviewed proposals.

The six earlier selections remain partial. Comuns–Front Ampli has 73 housing points covering its housing chapter and relevant cross-chapter measures; its other seven areas remain pending, and this is not a completed whole programme. Related commitments assigned to other areas remain part of that unfinished incorporation. BNG, Por un Mundo Más Justo, Falange Española de las JONS and Adelante Andalucía retain one selected proposal each while full incorporation continues. The PP second official download still needs comparison; byte equivalence between the supplied PSOE copy and both official URLs remains unverified. Frente Amplio's separate Galicia document was not obtained. Specialist language copyediting, particularly Basque and Aranese terminology, remains useful across selections. Frente Obrero's adopted political programme is not asserted to be a newly published electoral edition. These limits survive removal of historical review reports.

Programme incorporation remains unresolved for PCTE (no verified adoption of its older programme for this election), EH Bildu (exact election adoption of the consulted page), Junts per Catalunya (official source availability) and Alhora (campaign commitments do not establish a programme). These are current evidence gaps as of 22 November 2026, not claims that those formations have no programme.

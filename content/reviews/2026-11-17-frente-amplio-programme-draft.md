# Frente Amplio national programme — editorial review

Frente Amplio presented _Un programa como una casa_ in Madrid on 16 November 2026 for Spain's general election on 29 November. The national programme is the source for the congressional and senatorial election. The BOE confirms the election and records separately named territorial and Senate coalitions. This review uses Frente Amplio's existing Prisma identifier and makes no claim about the programme adopted by each joint list. ## External context checked on 17 November 2026

- The current Workers' Statute sets an ordinary maximum of 40 effective hours per week averaged over the year. The programme sets out a reduction in stages. [Workers' Statute, article 34](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430#a34)
- The housing law already establishes a procedure to declare stressed residential-market areas. The programme proposes its own rent ceiling and additional reduction within the applicable scope. [Housing Law 12/2023, article 18](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-12203#a18)
- Corporate income tax already has a statutory minimum-tax mechanism. The programme proposes a 20% effective minimum for specified large groups. [Corporate Income Tax Act, article 30 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328#a3-3)
- Social Security law already provides for annual adjustment of contributory pensions based on CPI. The programme proposes constitutional protection for indexation and a minimum linked to the statutory minimum wage. [General Social Security Act, article 58](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a58)
- The EU VAT Directive permits a domestic small-enterprise exemption with a threshold no higher than €85,000. The national programme proposes immediate transposition and specifies its proposed turnover cutoff and reporting obligations. [Council Directive (EU) 2020/285](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2020/285/oj?locale=es)
- PDF page 46, printed folio 51, proposes a marginal 100% rate on inheritance above €10 million per heir and includes an exemption for one home per heir. The exemption ends with a bare figure of 5 in the original render. The launch press release details additional brackets and rates, and does not resolve the unit of the PDF's exemption. The draft preserves the exemption without inferring the unit or importing rates from the press release. [Original, page 46](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=46) · [Official launch](https://frenteamplio.info/noticias/frente-amplio-propone-limitar-herencias-ultrarricos-maximo-10-millones-euros/)

### Source interpretation

- The public rent proposal and the stressed-market proposal use different bases for their 25% limits. The public rent text refers to household income. The private rent ceiling uses average net household income in the census section and the SERPAVI reference. [Original, page 10](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=10) · [Original, page 13](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=13)
- The child benefit states eligibility for dependants under 18 and gives a final rollout band ending in 18. The draft retains under-18 eligibility and the three-phase rollout. The fund allocation and the benefit's administration are distinct proposals. [Original, page 21](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=21) · [Original, page 62](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=62)
- The mental-health staffing proposal mentions several professions and one minimum of 10 professionals per 100,000 residents. The draft retains the single minimum without asserting separate quotas. [Original, page 28](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=28)
- The Income Support proposal in section 2.1 refers to 60% of the regional median in general and a 40% floor using the national median for young adults. Section 2.2 separately describes ending severe poverty using 40% of mean income. The draft follows section 2.1 for the proposal summarized here. [Original, page 22](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=22) · [Original, page 24](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=24)
- The campaign FAQ explicitly confirms the €800,000 combined-property condition for individuals under the housing accumulation tax. The PDF supplies the company's first-property rule and the exemptions. Wording follows those verifiable conditions. [Original, page 18](../sources/frente-amplio-2026-df6e4d967a3a/original.pdf#page=18) · [Official tax FAQ](https://frenteamplio.info/programa/impuesto-antiespeculacion/)

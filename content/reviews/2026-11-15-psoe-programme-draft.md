## External context checked on 15 November 2026

- Spanish law already ties annual adjustments of contributory pensions to CPI. Measure 100 proposes constitutional protection for indexation. [General Social Security Act, article 58](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#a58)
- The ordinary legal working-time maximum in the consolidated statute remains 40 effective hours per week averaged over the year. Measure 108 distinguishes a reduction to 37.5 hours from encouraging collective bargaining towards 35. [Workers' Statute, article 34](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430#a34)
- The 2026 minimum-wage decree refers to the 60% target. Measure 87 calls for a statutory floor above 60% of the average wage and addresses salary-supplement absorption. [Royal Decree 126/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-3815)
- Existing housing law sets out how competent housing authorities declare stressed residential-market areas. Measure 62 specifies temporary freezes for rents above reference indices in those markets. Measure 70 describes its own proposed tax on holdings across the page break. [Housing Law 12/2023, article 18](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-12203#a18)
- The existing energy and climate plan sets a 2030 target of 81% renewable electricity generation. The programme refers to that plan and its target. [PNIEC 2023–2030](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/energia/estrategia-normativa/pniec-23-30.html)
- The 2022 science law contains the 1.25% of GDP public research and development objective for 2030. Measure 8 proposes raising the target to 1.5% by 2035. [Science Law 17/2022](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2022/09/05/17)
- Law 4/2026 already amends disability and dependency law. Measures 205–206 concern full implementation and consolidation of home help. [Law 4/2026](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2026/10/01/4)
- The current law protects access to voluntary termination of pregnancy under statutory terms. Measure 368 commits to the highest legal protection for it, marriage equality and euthanasia. Its introductory section frames the commitments as constitutional and legal reform. [Organic Law 2/2010](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/lo/2010/03/03/2)

## Editorial decision

On 15 November 2026, the maintainer approved the exact 32-point batch in all six languages and authorised publication. The eight syntheses advanced from pending revision 1 to partial revision 2 at `2026-11-15T19:13:59Z`. The selection leaves the remaining numbered commitments and any territorial supplements available for later review. The approved wording preserves the proposed `unión de amistad` as a mutual-care legal arrangement. Technical terms in Basque and Aranese received fidelity review. Future language suggestions can use the same review process.

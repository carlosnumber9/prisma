# Podemos national programme editorial review

On 17 November 2026 Podemos presented _Así de básico: Paz, casa y trabajo_ for Spain's 29 November general election. The official campaign page identifies it as the general-election programme and links directly to the full PDF. The party's announcement is visible in an archived copy because X returned HTTP 403. The time of the announcement does not establish when the exact PDF bytes became available. Royal Decree 806/2026 was signed on 5 October and published on 6 October. It calls elections to Congress and Senate in 52 and 60 constituencies respectively, with separate Senate constituencies for Ibiza and Formentera. The 3 November proclamation lists Podemos candidatures and separately names Compromís: Front Valencià and Unidad del Pueblo Canario. This review covers Podemos's national programme. ## External context checked on 18 November 2026

- The Urban Leases Act already sets limits for certain new residential leases while a declared stressed-market zone is in force. The national programme proposes an extraordinary reduction with a different scope. [Urban Leases Act, article 17](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1994-26003#a17)
- The Workers' Statute sets an ordinary maximum of 40 effective hours per week averaged over the year. The programme's shorter working week is a proposed change. [Workers' Statute, article 34](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11430#a34)
- The Council of Ministers approved a monthly minimum wage of €1,286 for 2027 on 17 November, effective from 1 January 2027 according to its announcement. The programme's own amount and implementation terms govern the draft wording. [Council of Ministers reference](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/consejodeministros/referencias/Paginas/2026/20261117-referencia-rueda-de-consejo-ministros.aspx)
- The existing corporate income tax minimum generally applies to groups that file on a consolidated basis and taxpayers that meet the €20 million turnover threshold, with statutory adjustments and exceptions. The programme changes both the effective rate and the calculation base. [Corporate Income Tax Act, article 30 bis](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328#a3-3)
- Ordinary retirement age under the 2026 transitional schedule depends on the contribution record. The programme proposes a different ordinary retirement age and a separate change to early retirement penalties. [General Social Security Act, seventh transitional provision](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-11724#dtseptima)
- The Climate Change and Energy Transition Act sets a national climate-neutrality horizon before 2050. The programme proposes advancing the horizon to 2040. [Climate Change and Energy Transition Act, article 3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-8447#a3)

These checks establish the regulatory context for the proposal text. They do not establish its cost, feasibility, or likely outcomes.

## Editorial selection and open questions

The initial selection has 24 proposals, with three per existing policy area. It includes prominent campaign commitments when the programme gives specific wording, and uses the existing institutions and rights area for the chosen defence commitment. The original retains all 442 measures. Broader sections such as culture, migration and equality remain only partly represented or outside this selection.

- Housing measure 1 makes the 40% reduction conditional on the resulting price reaching at least 60% of the current local average. It separately sets the initial price for a home never previously rented out. The programme gives no fallback formula for a previously rented home whose 40% reduction would fail that condition. [Official PDF, p. 8](https://pazcasaytrabajo.es/programa-electoral/programa-electoral-paz-casa-y-trabajo.pdf#page=8)
- Measure 328 gives a 50% figure for fossil primary energy by 2030 without identifying the baseline or denominator. The draft uses the electricity generation and decarbonisation targets for 2035 and 2040, together with the gas backup and employment provisions. [Official PDF, p. 98](https://pazcasaytrabajo.es/programa-electoral/programa-electoral-paz-casa-y-trabajo.pdf#page=98)
- Measure 427 uses the common name _Ley Mordaza_ in its heading. Its body refers to repealing a reform of the organic law on citizen security. The draft keeps the programme's common name and proposed measures. The exact legislative instrument covered by the repeal remains an interpretation question. [Official PDF, p. 133](https://pazcasaytrabajo.es/programa-electoral/programa-electoral-paz-casa-y-trabajo.pdf#page=133) · [Organic Law 4/2015](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2015-3442)

Specialist review remains open for Basque and Aranese terminology, particularly for expropriation of use, legal personality, public-sector employment, the constitutional terms and treaty withdrawal. The education stages, working-hours deadline and statutory co-funding terms also warrant a terminology check across locales.

## QA and decision

The native Research profile verified the national source and formation. Synthesis prepared the first selection and all six locale drafts in an isolated copy of the existing catalogue. Independent QA checked the electoral originals, programme provenance, source hashes, all 24 points and their translations. Its findings were applied to the wording for rent reduction, educational and health co-funding, the energy targets and the constitutional proposal. The final 60 quotations match the retained extraction and independent PDFKit text on the cited physical pages after whitespace normalisation. The four generated files passed deterministic verification. They retain all 159 previously approved proposals and show eight pending Podemos entries with no public proposals or source references in the isolated output.

# PSOE programme discovery — 14 November 2026

This review records a nationwide search for the PSOE federal programme, official sections and supplements for the Spanish general election of 29 November 2026. It covers Congress and Senate and includes PSOE federations and PSC publications relevant to that election. The consultation took place on 14 November, after the official campaign opened. Article 2 of [Real Decreto 806/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20742) convokes both chambers, and article 4 dates the campaign from 13 to 27 November. The [3 November proclamation](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-23103) supports the electoral context. Candidature research has its [own review](../political-info/reviews/2026-10-30-psoe-lists-discovery.md).

## Federal findings

The search did not locate a complete PSOE programme, an official release date or an adopted programme section or annex for the 29 November election. This finding applies to the sources checked on 14 November. Later publication and material outside the checked surfaces remain possible.

- The [programme archive](https://www.psoe.es/transparencia/informacion-politica-organizativa/programa/) lists the June 2024 European election first. July 2023 is the latest general-election entry. The [generic programme path](https://www.psoe.es/programa-electoral/) serves _Ahora progreso_ for the general election of 10 November 2019.
- The [federal homepage](https://www.psoe.es/?sfnsn=scwspmo) links to [Electo PSOE](https://electopsoe.psoe.es/) for voting instructions and to the one-page [campaign committee chart](https://www.psoe.es/media-content/2026/11/ORGANIGRAMA-COMITE-ELECTORAL.pdf), which names Cristina Narbona under “Programa electoral” and identifies the 29 November election.
- The reviewed [documents approved by PSOE bodies](https://www.psoe.es/actualidad/documentos-de-interes/) and [federal news](https://www.psoe.es/actualidad/noticias-actualidad/) list standalone manifestos and campaign news. The [November agenda](https://www.psoe.es/agenda/11/2026/), [14 November Palma campaign statement](https://www.psoe.es/actualidad/noticias-actualidad/pedro-sanchez-la-buena-politica-es-avanzar-y-la-mala-educacion-es-involucionar-y-la-representan-pp-y-vox/) and [El Socialista](https://www.psoe.es/el-socialista/) supply campaign events, remarks and editorials. No official programme download or launch notice for this election was located across these checks.
- [Seguridad ciudadana](https://appdoc.psoe.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SEGURIDAD-CIUDADANA-CONVIVENCIA-NUEVOS-DERECHOS.pdf) is a ten-page PSOE campaign brief bearing the printed date 22 October 2026, the `29N26` branding and the heading “6 Convivencia y nuevos derechos”. Pages 1–3 introduce talking points and review government activity. Pages 4–10 mix objectives and challenges with descriptions of existing policies. Its publication date remains unverified.

The security brief is the principal federal lead. The document and the reviewed public campaign surfaces did not identify it as an adopted programme chapter or annex. Targeted queries for the exact filename, campaign branding and an official index did not establish a parent publication or the rest of the numbered series. Direct capture in the workspace failed DNS resolution. The browser-readable PDF therefore supplies page locators and campaign context. No original file, SHA-256 or verified extraction enters the programme corpus from that preview.

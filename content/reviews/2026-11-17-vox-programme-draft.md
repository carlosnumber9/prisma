## External context

The following sources were consulted on 17 November 2026 between 21:41 and 21:52 UTC. They establish the current legal and administrative context. The exact proposal wording comes from the national programme.

- The first-home measure proposes a zero VAT rate on qualifying new homes and a separate 100% ITP rebate for qualifying second-hand homes. Article 91 currently sets a general 10% VAT rate for dwellings and a 4% rate for specified protected dwellings. Articles 98 and 105a of the EU VAT Directive restrict sub-5% and zero-rating options. The published programme gives no route for implementing a zero rate of the scope proposed. [VAT Act, article 91](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-28740#a91) · [EU VAT Directive, articles 98 and 105a](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A02006L0112-20250414)
- The proposed eviction deadline is under 24 hours. The current rapid-trial procedure for certain usurpation and unlawful-entry offences depends on procedural conditions. The Public Prosecutor's Office sets out different routes for the different offences. [Public Prosecutor's Office Circular 1/2025](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-17153)
- The current minimum-income law generally requires legal and effective residence for the preceding year, subject to exceptions, as well as financial vulnerability. The programme proposes restricting the benefit to eligible Spaniards. Article 24 of the EU free-movement directive provides for equal treatment for qualifying residents and specifies exceptions. The effect of a proposed rule would depend on the legal text and affected categories. [Minimum income act, article 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2021-21007#a10) · [Free movement directive, article 24](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2004/38/oj?locale=es)
- The current immigration law permits temporary residence on the basis of arraigo and also provides other exceptional grounds. The proposal explicitly calls for removing the arraigo route. [Immigration act, article 31.3](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2000-544#a31)
- The Ministry of Health describes health cards issued by the autonomous communities that are already linked through a national identifier, and a mechanism for sharing specified clinical records. The programme proposes a single card and shared records across the system. [Individual health card](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/areas/saludDigital/tarjetaSanitariaSNS/home.htm) · [Digital clinical record](https://www.sanidad.gob.es/profesionales/hcdsns/Preguntas_frecuentes.htm)
- Article 3 ter of the National Health System Act provides for care for people without legal residence under specified conditions. The programme calls for excluding foreign nationals in an irregular situation except in serious emergencies. [National Health System Act, article 3 ter](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-10715#a3ter)
- Measure 42 proposes nationwide recognition without additional procedures, licences or registrations. Law 20/2013 already addresses market unity. Constitutional Court judgment 79/2017 annulled several of its original provisions on nationwide effectiveness, including articles 19 and 20. The programme leaves the legislative route unspecified. [Market Unity Act](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2013-12888#a19) · [Constitutional Court judgment 79/2017](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2017-8464)
- The programme describes the vehicle rules as a ban on combustion engines. Article 1(5a) of Regulation (EU) 2019/631 sets a 100% fleet-wide CO₂ reduction target from 2035 against the 2021 target for new passenger cars and light commercial vehicles. The EU legislative file for the Commission's proposed 90% target was still awaiting a committee decision when checked. [Consolidated fleet emissions regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02019R0631-20250709) · [European Parliament procedure 2025/0420](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2025%2F0420%28COD%29)

## Editorial questions

- The self-employment proposal gives a €0 contribution below the minimum wage and a progressive contribution from €50 above it. The text leaves the treatment of income exactly at the minimum wage unspecified. [PDF, page 9, measure 2](../sources/vox-2026-065abf6720be/original.pdf#page=9)
- The birth-rate plan is framed as support for Spanish families. Its first item refers to extending the existing large-family benefits to all families progressively from the first child. The approved wording keeps both formulations. [PDF, page 51, measure 94](../sources/vox-2026-065abf6720be/original.pdf#page=51)
- The programme specifies repatriation for people who remain in an irregular situation and a plan to facilitate return under separate criteria. The approved wording preserves both commitments. [PDF, page 15, measures 12–13](../sources/vox-2026-065abf6720be/original.pdf#page=15)
- The nuclear proposal calls for studies at available sites adjacent to existing plants and for proceeding with projects found viable. It gives no plant count or commissioning date. [PDF, page 12, measure 8](../sources/vox-2026-065abf6720be/original.pdf#page=12)

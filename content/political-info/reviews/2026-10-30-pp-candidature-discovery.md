# PP candidature discovery — 30 October 2026

## Request, scope and execution

Investigate the PP's lists for the Spanish general election of 29 November 2026, Congress and Senate, nationwide. The maintainer explicitly fixed that election as the project's permanent scope; future requests do not require another election-selection question. This run uses the separate political-reference contract and does not incorporate a programme.

Builder directly invoked the native named profiles `prisma-research` and `prisma-qa`. Research discovered and extracted national electoral publications; Builder investigated official formation announcements and preserved their evidence; QA independently reopened official sources and audited every extracted candidate row. Programme Synthesis was not invoked because its documented role excludes candidature conversion. No recurring automation, approved content, application projection or Git publication was requested.

The electoral cutoff is 30 October 2026. Actual source captures range from 2026-10-30T15:14:28Z to 2026-10-30T15:19:18.498611Z. These consultation timestamps do not establish availability before publication or exclude a later change on the same day.

QA checked the amendment's publication on 2 July and entry into force on 3 July. The actual candidature publication enumerates 60 Senate constituencies: 47 peninsular provinces, 11 islands and two autonomous cities. Do not substitute a historical constituency universe.

The BOE and UPN nomination identify Casto Alvero, while the PP PDF prints Casto Albero. BOE wording is retained for the submitted-stage inventory; neither an inferred legal-name correction nor a silent harmonisation was made. Builder rendered and inspected physical page 40 of the PP PDF; Research rendered BOE physical pages 34, 149 and 151; QA independently rendered BOE pages 2, 4, 34 and 149. Not every PDF page was visually reviewed.

Its shortened public names do not override the BOE spelling.

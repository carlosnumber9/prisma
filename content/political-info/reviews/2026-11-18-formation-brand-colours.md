# Brand colours for the five programme formations

This follow-up to the [reference investigation](2026-11-18-five-formation-reference-discovery.md) checks exact digital colours on 18 November 2026. It covers the institutional identity of PP, PSOE, VOX and Podemos, and the 29N coalition branding published by Frente Amplio.

The VOX and Podemos hex codes are the RGB values stored in official wordmark images. The Pantone numbers express the specifications given in the statutes. The review inspected the two embedded wordmarks visually, checked the stored channel values and confirmed the quoted specifications on the indicated physical pages. Podemos's documents page describes a 2025 assembly document set, but direct access to that page returned HTTP 403. The statutory text cited here is the edition the national homepage currently links. The October consultation corroborates the purple in use.

The existing catalogue holds the PP and PSOE colours. This research records evidence for the three additional formations. Its scope is verification and source preservation, with the application accent for Frente Amplio still to be selected for any catalogue update. Three new originals and their extracts were preserved. Hash verification passed for those captures and seven reused snapshots. On 18 November 2026 the maintainer approved the colour findings and authorized committing and pushing the review and preserved evidence to `master`.

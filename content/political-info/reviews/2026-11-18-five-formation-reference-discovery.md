# Political reference discovery for five formations

The 18 November 2026 run covers Partido Popular (PP), Partido Socialista Obrero Español (PSOE), VOX, Frente Amplio and Podemos, the five formations with approved programme selections in Prisma. It covers all 52 Congress and 60 Senate constituencies for the 29 November general election. A campaign nomination for the presidency, a party office and a position on a constituency list are distinct claims. [Election call and annex](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20742)

Builder assigned one native Research agent the shared national electoral source, two native Research agents the formation profiles, and a native QA agent the independent audit. The profile scopes were PP, PSOE and VOX, and Frente Amplio and Podemos. Each role received a bounded task packet. The election and published programme files provided the scope and stable formation IDs. There was no programme synthesis task.

## Electoral evidence

The national reference point is the 3 November BOE proclamation and its 7 November correction. Builder inspected all 14 available BOE summary XML documents from 3 to 18 November inclusive. The API returned HTTP 404 for Sundays 8 and 15 November. The 7 November item was the sole matching national correction in these summaries. The check covers national BOE publication through the cutoff. Separate local notices, litigation and changes not published in the BOE remain outside this check. [Proclamation](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-23103) · [Correction](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-23383) · [JEC electoral inventory](https://www.juntaelectoralcentral.es/cs/jec/elecciones/coalicionesJECGenerales)

The component names in the JEC acronym differ from those in the campaign announcement, which mentions Ceuta Ya. The JEC entry establishes the registered form and the BOE establishes the proclaimed names and order. Any person's party membership requires separate evidence. ## Formation profiles and brand evidence

- **Official websites.** [PP](https://www.pp.es/), [PSOE](https://www.psoe.es/), [VOX](https://www.voxespana.es/), [Frente Amplio](https://frenteamplio.info/) and [Podemos](https://podemos.info/). The latter also uses a separate [29N campaign website](https://pazcasaytrabajo.es/).

- **Partido Popular.** The July 2025 statutes describe a national party with regional, provincial or island, district and local organisation. Alberto Núñez Feijóo is the party president and first proclaimed candidate for Congress in Madrid. The homepage uses a short logo with fill `#21599E`. A national identity manual and licensed official font remain unverified. Continued use of that manual and web embedding rights remain unverified. ## Preservation and checks

The run preserved 38 new snapshots under `content/political-info/sources`, including the 14 national daily indexes, the JEC coalition inventory, official electoral originals and relevant formation material. The preservation check verified both SHA-256 values for each snapshot. PDF segment counts match all 356 physical pages in the four preserved PDFs. The PP statutes are image based and the text extract contains only the transcribed evidence from physical page 5 and any embedded text. Capture failures for the VOX and federal PSOE sources are recorded above, alongside evidence from the official rendered copies. The research did not distribute the downloaded Frente Amplio font. Its website use establishes neither a coalition-wide specification nor permission to provide it as a Prisma asset.

QA independently reconstructed the electoral observations from the official HTML. All 459 scoped entries matched the XML-derived observations. These include 456 proclaimed lists with 4,043 candidate appearances and three explicitly unproclaimed entries. The audit checked 8,321 row and name locators and found no differences in candidate order, positions, substitute assignments or controlled list scope. It also checked the six relevant correction blocks and the related Tarragona Senate ordinal swap. The raw observations and correction summary remain in the temporary run directory. Reusable originals are preserved in the repository.

The source signature scan, documentation and formatting checks, programme projection check and 115 deterministic tests passed locally. The programme check verifies the existing programme pipeline. On 18 November 2026 the maintainer approved the findings and authorized committing and pushing the review and preserved evidence to `master`. The political-reference JSON dataset, app integration, reconciliation of individual identities across lists and representation of titular-specific Senate substitutes in the public contract remain pending.
